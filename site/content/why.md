## Why STE

ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English (STE) is a controlled language. It started in the aerospace industry, for maintenance documents. Its writing rules give a writer a small set of words and short sentences. Each approved word has one meaning.

- **Fewer mistakes.** A reader who does a task from a document needs one meaning for each word. A short instruction in the imperative is clear.
- **Readers of all languages.** Many readers of a project do not speak English as their first language. Short sentences and common words are easier to read and to translate.
- **Faster reviews.** A tool finds many problems before a person examines the text. The person can then examine the meaning.

The linter applies the writing rules to the README files, the documents and the doc comments of a project. It is an approximation of the standard, not a full check. A text with no findings is not "STE compliant", and a person must examine the text too.

The text of this page obeys the rules: CI examines it with the same linter.
